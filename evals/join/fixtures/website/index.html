Ewan MacCallister

Close-up & parlour magician · Edinburgh, Scotland

About

Ewan MacCallister is a close-up magician working out of Leith, Edinburgh. For the past fourteen years he has performed sleight-of-hand with cards, coins and borrowed objects at weddings, ceilidhs, corporate dinners and private parties from the Borders to the Outer Hebrides.

His style is quiet and conversational. He prefers a table of six to a theatre of six hundred, and he would rather you remember a single impossible moment than a dozen flashy ones.

“Ewan sat down at our table for ten minutes and my father-in-law still talks about it three years later.” — wedding guest, Perthshire

Repertoire

Signature pieces include a card-to-wallet routine performed with a borrowed deck, a coins-across sequence using pre-decimal Scottish pennies, and a mentalism closer built around the audience’s own handwriting. Ewan is also happy to build a bespoke piece around a company logo, a couple’s story, or a product launch.
